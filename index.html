About

One person,
the whole record.

I'm Osadebey, a Lagos-based music producer, mixing and mastering engineer and recording artiste. Over the last few years I've built a catalogue of 120+ credits with 187 collaborators. That includes co-producing on TY Bello's I Speak Lion and working with Naffymar, Oba Reengy, Spirit of Prophecy, TBabz, Pawasonic and CalledOut Music.

My home is worship and gospel, but my ear travels: Afro-pop, street records, acoustic sessions, even runway sound design. Because I produce, engineer and perform, I hear a song the way the artist does and the way the listener will.
